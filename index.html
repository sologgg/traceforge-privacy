TraceForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

TraceForge is a Chrome extension for developers to inspect, edit and replay HTTP requests, view page storage, and manage local cookie profiles for sites they build or test.

Data the extension handles

Only when you start a capture or use a feature in the extension, TraceForge processes data for the website you are viewing: request and response URLs, headers and bodies, cookies, and page storage (localStorage/sessionStorage) and form structure. This may include authentication data (such as cookies or tokens), web browsing activity and personal information contained in requests or pages.

How data is stored

All data is stored only on your device, in Chrome's extension storage. Captured requests for a tab are cleared when that tab is closed. Saved requests, cookie profiles, discovered action templates and settings remain on your device until you delete them in the extension or uninstall it.

Sharing

TraceForge does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising. Data leaves your browser only when you choose to replay a request (it is sent to that request's own URL) or copy something to your clipboard.

Limited use

We do not sell user data, transfer it to third parties, use it for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
